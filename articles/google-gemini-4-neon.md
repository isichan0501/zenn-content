[November 18, 2026] Google announced Gemini 4 Neon, a new frontier model bringing fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out immediately in the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, and for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and Google Antigravity.

According to Google, Gemini 4 Neon delivers frontier-level intelligence with particular strength in rapid task execution. The model is positioned between the long-horizon reasoning models (Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon) and everyday models, offering fast performance for software engineering and knowledge work tasks. Google reports that Hydrogen, a related variant, scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at the default high thinking level, versus 50 for Argon and 54 at the xhigh setting.

Google also introduced Orchestrator in Antigravity, which switches between models such as Carbon and Hydrogen within a session. In benchmarking, Google reports near Carbon task success on Terminal-Bench 4.0 with 24% less quota use and 19% faster completion.

The model is available through AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, and Google Antigravity. Pricing for Hydrogen is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, flat across a 1M-token context window.

**Primary source**: [Google blog: Introducing Gemini 4 Neon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/)
